## 📚 Grammar — Part 1

### Fundamentals of Grammar

**Grammar** is a set of rules and regulations.

Grammar is needed for writing.

```text
Alphabets → Words → Grammar
```

Words are categorised into **8 sections / parts of speech** in Standard English.

---

## 🧠 8 Parts of Speech

### 1. Noun

A **noun** is the name of a:

- Person
- Place
- Thing
- Animal
- etc.

### 2. Pronoun

A **pronoun** is used **in place of a noun**.

### 3. Verb

A **verb** represents **action words**.

Examples noted in the material include:

- Main verb
- Auxiliary verb

### 4. Adjective

An **adjective** describes a **quality** or gives extra information about a **noun or pronoun**.

### 5. Adverb

An **adverb** gives **extra information about the verb**.

### 6. Preposition

A **preposition** is a word used before a:

- Noun
- Noun phrase
- Pronoun

### 7. Conjunction

A **conjunction** is a **connecting / joining word**.

### 8. Interjection

An **interjection** is used to express **emotions**.
